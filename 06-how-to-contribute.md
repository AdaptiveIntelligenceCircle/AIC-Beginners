# 06 — How to contribute

Contributions are welcome when they respect the under-claim posture, the written principles, and the ownership of each repository.

---

## Principles for contributors

1. **Under-claim in public text**  
   Do not add marketing, guarantees, or “soon mainnet” language. Prefer criteria, evidence, and limits.

2. **Fail-closed bias**  
   Changes that expand automated power, weaken human gates, or soften recovery requirements need stronger justification and review than changes that clarify or restrict.

3. **Entity ≠ immunity**  
   Do not write docs that imply legal privilege, exemption from regulation, or founder exceptionalism.

4. **No token rule-power**  
   Do not introduce governance or ethical authority that depends on token balances or market mechanisms.

5. **Small, reviewable steps**  
   Prefer PRs that one reviewer can understand in a single sitting. Large redesigns belong in design discussions first.

6. **Write to the repository that owns the concern**  
   Orientation → Start-Here or principles. Kernel behavior → ethical-kernel / TestNet area. Covenant criteria → Covenant repo. Do not scatter binding claims.

---

## Practical workflow

1. **Read** the target repository’s README, CONTRIBUTING (if any), and related principle docs.
2. **Search** existing issues and PRs before opening duplicates.
3. **Open an issue** for design questions or large changes; use a PR for concrete, scoped fixes.
4. **Reference** existing terms (glossary) and constraints (is-and-is-not, Covenant criteria) in the description.
5. **Accept** that “not merging” is a valid outcome when a change conflicts with fail-closed or under-claim posture.

---

## Types of contribution that fit well early

- Documentation clarity and consistency with principles
- Tests (unit, property, smoke) for existing code
- Glossary and FAQ improvements
- Accessibility and correctness of the public site (without hype)
- Observability and audit-event sketches aligned with transparency policy
- Translations **only if** meaning is preserved and under-claim language is not softened

---

## Types of contribution that need extra care (or are out of scope)

- Anything that could be read as a Covenant declaration or mainnet announcement
- Token, fundraising, or “rule by stake” designs
- Weakening of human gates, recovery, or disclosure requirements
- Claims about compliance with specific laws without legal review (see safety-and-law)
- Personal attacks or cult-like framing of individuals

---

## Code of conduct

See [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).  
Disagreement about design is expected; harassment and bad-faith over-claim pressure are not.

---

## License

Contributions are expected to be compatible with the repository LICENSE (GPL-3.0-or-later family unless a specific repo states otherwise). Do not submit code you cannot license under those terms.
