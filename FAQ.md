# FAQ

Short answers. When a short answer is insufficient, the linked orientation files are authoritative over improvisation.

---

## Status and claims

**Is AIC live / mainnet / Covenant-declared?**  
No. The project is pre-Covenant. Criteria and checklists may exist as documents; a formal declaration that they are satisfied has not been made in the strong sense described in Covenant materials.

**Is there a token?**  
Design rule: no token rule-power. Do not assume a governance or ethical role for any token.

**Is this a DAO that rules by vote weight or stake?**  
No. Token or stake-based rule-power is explicitly rejected in the design orientation.

---

## What AIC is

**Is AIC a blockchain?**  
It includes TestNet-oriented and protocol-design work, but it is not accurately reduced to “a blockchain project.” Ethical constraints, human gates, and under-claim narrative are central.

**Is AIC a religion or ideology with a leader who cannot be questioned?**  
No. Entity ≠ immunity. Critique of design and of individuals in their project roles is expected to stay within the code of conduct.

**Can I treat chat messages from founders or contributors as binding policy?**  
No. Prefer written documents in the repositories. Informal chat is not a substitute for criteria and review.

## Contributing

**I am new. What should I do first?**  
Read this repository in order (01 → 07), then [05-first-week.md](05-first-week.md).

**Can I open a large architectural PR in week 1?**  
Discouraged. Start with documentation, tests, or a scoped issue. Design changes need discussion against existing principles.

**Where do I report a security concern?**  
Use the SECURITY process of the relevant repository. Do not file public issues that enable exploitation.

---

## Law and safety

**Does participating give legal protection?**  
No. Entity ≠ immunity. Ordinary law applies.

**Does AIC claim compliance with the EU AI Act / Vietnam AI rules / other regimes?**  
No automatic compliance claim. Legal policy notes are orientation. Obtain qualified advice for real deployments.

**Is experimental code safe for production?**  
Do not treat it as production-ready. Fail-closed and under-claim posture forbid that assumption.

---

## Narrative discipline

**Someone is promoting AIC with strong guarantees. What should I do?**  
Point to [02-is-and-is-not.md](02-is-and-is-not.md) and principle docs. Prefer correcting the public record with references over amplifying hype.

**Can I translate materials?**  
Yes if meaning and under-claim language are preserved. Softening constraints in translation is not acceptable.
