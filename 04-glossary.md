# 04 — Glossary (working)

Short definitions for terms used across AIC public materials.  
These are **working** definitions for orientation. Formal specs may refine them.

---

## A–C

**AIC (Adaptive Intelligence Circle)**  
The overall effort: open design, documentation, and experimental implementation of ethical coordination and meaning-infrastructure ideas under an under-claim, fail-closed posture.

**Covenant**  
A declared state in which published criteria (technical, governance, disclosure, recovery, human gates, etc.) are asserted to have been met. Until declaration, the project remains **pre-Covenant**.

**Ethical Kernel**  
A small core of constraints and procedures intended to be difficult to bypass by design. Not a marketing slogan; treated as something that must be testable and reviewable.

---

## E–H

**Entity ≠ immunity**  
Principle that any legal entity, organization, or founder role does not grant exemption from law, critique, or responsibility.

**Fail-closed**  
Default behavior under uncertainty or missing evidence: restrict or refuse rather than expand capability or authority.

**Human Meaning Network (HMN)**  
Design area concerned with keeping human judgment, recovery, and meaning-related gates explicit rather than fully automated away.

**Human gate**  
A required human decision or confirmation point in a process that must not be silently bypassed by automation.

---

## N–P

**No token rule-power**  
Design rule: tokens (or market mechanisms) must not become the source of governance or ethical authority.

**Parallel nets / net_id**  
TestNet concept: multiple nets identified by `net_id`, with bridges default-deny, to limit accidental coupling and blast radius.

**Pre-Covenant**  
Current status: Covenant criteria may be written, but no formal public declaration that they are satisfied and binding in the strong sense.

**Public Digital Infrastructure (PDI)**  
Policy orientation: treating certain coordination and meaning infrastructure as public-interest digital infrastructure subject to transparency and legal constraints, not pure private capture.

---

## R–T

**Recovery / RecoveryOrchestrator**  
Mechanisms and components concerned with restoring safe operation after faults, key issues, or policy triggers—without silent expansion of power.

**Resonance / Emergence / Transcendence**  
Named design phases. They describe intended progression of architecture and constraints, not automatic calendar milestones.

**SSI (bind / revoke / rotate)**  
Self-sovereign identity related operations: binding, revocation, and rotation of credentials or keys under explicit policy.

**Third Path**  
Orientation that tries to avoid pure state capture *and* pure market/token capture of coordination rules, while remaining accountable to law and human recovery.

**TransparencyDashboard**  
Public-facing structure for audit events, metrics snapshots, and transparency policy—aimed at observability of process, not at hype metrics.

---

## U–W

**Under-claim**  
Narrative and documentation discipline: say less than the maximum possible interpretation; prefer evidence and criteria over aspiration in public statements.

**World Meaning Infrastructure (WMI)**  
Design direction for infrastructure that supports shared meaning and coordination under ethical constraints. Not a claim that such infrastructure already exists globally.

---

## Usage notes

- Prefer linking to this glossary or to primary principle docs when introducing terms in new documents.
- If a term’s meaning drifts in chat, correct toward the written definition and open an issue if the written definition itself needs update.
- Do not invent new capital-letter concepts without a short written definition and a clear owner repository.
