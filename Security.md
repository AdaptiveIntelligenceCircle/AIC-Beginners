# Security Policy

AIC prioritizes security as part of its ethical covenant.

## Reporting Vulnerabilities
- Please report security issues responsibly.
- Contact: https://opencollective.com/adaptive-intelligence-circle
- Do not disclose vulnerabilities publicly until they are acknowledged and addressed.

## Scope
- All repositories under Adaptive Intelligence Circle.
- Zero-data principle: no personal data is collected or stored.

## Commitment
- We respond to reports within 7 days.
- Fixes will be documented transparently.
- Security is treated as a collective responsibility.
