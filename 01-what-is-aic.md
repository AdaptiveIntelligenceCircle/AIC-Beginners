# 01 — What is AIC?

**Short answer**

Adaptive Intelligence Circle (AIC) is an independent, open, non-profit-oriented effort to design and document an **ethical coordination protocol** and supporting **World Meaning Infrastructure** ideas. It emphasizes human meaning, fail-closed behavior, and explicit limits on automated power.

It is **not** a launched blockchain, **not** a token project, and **not** a claim that any current software is ready for high-stakes production use.

---

## Core orientation (under-claim)

AIC materials repeatedly state:

- **Third Path**: an attempt to avoid both pure state capture and pure market/token capture of coordination rules.
- **Ethical Kernel**: a small set of constraints and procedures that are intended to be hard to bypass by design, not by marketing.
- **Human Meaning Network (HMN)**: mechanisms that keep human judgment and recovery paths explicit rather than fully automated away.
- **No token rule-power**: tokens (if any ever appear) must not become the source of governance or ethical authority.
- **Pre-Covenant**: until explicit, published Covenant criteria are met and declared, nothing is “live” in the strong sense.
- **Entity ≠ immunity**: any organization or legal form does not shield participants from law, critique, or responsibility.
- **Fail-closed**: when in doubt, restrict or refuse rather than expand capability.

These are design commitments and narrative constraints. They are not guarantees of success.

---

## What exists today

Public materials include (among others):

- Orientation and principle documents
- Phase architectures (Emergence, Resonance, Transcendence, Covenant criteria)
- TestNet-oriented C++ / CMake structures and testing notes
- Transparency and policy sketches
- Website and documentation sites

Much of this is **specification, design, and experimental code**, not production infrastructure.

---

## What AIC is trying to protect against (high level)

- Coordination systems that become captured by the strongest token holders or the strongest state actors without residual human accountability.
- “Meaning” and ethical language being used as cover for expanded automated power without corresponding constraints.
- Romantic or messianic narratives that outrun evidence and process.

The response is deliberately conservative in claims and conservative in default behavior.

---

## What you should not assume

- That there is a mainnet.
- That there is a binding Covenant in force.
- That any current component has been formally verified end-to-end for adversarial environments.
- That participation creates legal privilege or immunity.
- That informal statements by any individual override written under-claim documents.
