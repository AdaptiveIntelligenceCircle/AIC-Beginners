# Code of Conduct

Adaptive Intelligence Circle (AIC) is committed to building an ethical, inclusive, and respectful community.

## Principles
- Respect all contributors regardless of background, identity, or perspective.
- Uphold the covenant: ethical-from-kernel, zero-donation, zero-data.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Collaboration must remain constructive and transparent.

## Enforcement
- Violations may result in warnings or removal from participation.
- Decisions are guided by the covenant, not by financial or political interests.

By contributing, you agree to uphold this Code of Conduct.
